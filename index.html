About me

I’m a graphic designer and communicator who blends editorial rigour, visual clarity and playful experimentation.

I turn complex information, from statistical handbooks to investment reports, into publications, infographics, web content and videos people want to read and watch, in English, French and Arabic.

I want my work to help people recognise emerging threats and act on the evidence: clear, careful and open to every audience.

Based in Geneva · EN · FR · العربية

Orangerie

Why it matters for UNODCA full season of events carried across website, print and social media in one voice, from the first announcement to the last show.
Feed
Story
Carousel
Video
Interview
Théâtre de l’Orangerie is an open-air theatre in Geneva’s Parc La Grange. With the head of communications, I built the social media strategy, and I published the season’s programme and events on the website, so each part of the summer came across online in one recognisable voice.
Feed posts, stories, carousels and promo videos, all designed on one identity so the whole season feels coherent, from the first announcement to the last.
I pushed for us to make videos of the concerts, and they turned out to be what drove the most engagement.

Not Just Another Fashion Podcast

Why it matters for UNODCCover art, an animated intro and social teasers: one concept adapted across every format, motion included.
intro.mp4
Visual identity for Not Just Another Fashion Podcast, a show that speculates on a sustainable fashion industry that doesn’t exist yet. “Another” repeats in a new typeface every time, like the clothes sold to us again in a different font.
